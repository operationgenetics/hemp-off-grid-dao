4 · ML-DSA-65 verifier — the post-quantum half

Deploys the verifier for your robot's PQC public key, then proves its cache on-chain. ExpandA(rho) and H(pk) are recomputed by the contract and rejected unless they match — nobody, including you, can install a forged matrix. Four signatures: deploy, commitTr, three chunk commits, seal. Roughly 20M gas per chunk, which fits an Arbitrum block. Do this before revoking.

5 · setupRoomieRobotAndLock

Callable immediately, and re-callable every time you get new hardware, until you revoke. Only 0xaF57…fB8e may call it. Public keys only — biometric templates stay on the MCU and must never be entered here.

6 · revokeAndFinalize — one way, permanent

Destroys the admin role forever. After this no address can change anything, ever. Refused unless a real PQC key (≥1312 bytes), a reserve address and a working PQC verifier are registered, so no sealed DAO can ever rest on secp256k1 alone. Do this only after the robot is in hand and its real key is on-chain.
